About

Engineers who build your software, fix it, and stick around.

We're a team of PEC-registered software engineers. We build management systems and AI agents for businesses around the world, rescue projects other teams left behind, and look after all of it for the long run. Remotely most days, and in person when the job needs hands on hardware.

Why we started

Most businesses we meet still run on a mix of notebooks, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and software nobody quite trusts. Staff lose hours answering the same messages and copying numbers from one place to another. And far too many own an app whose developer simply vanished.

We started NextGen Tech Solutions to fix that. Management systems that keep a business's data in one reliable place. Agents that handle the repetitive conversations and the invisible back-office work. And a team that stays, because software that's abandoned after launch stops being useful within a year.

We're engineers first. We write plain proposals, give fixed quotes, show working software every two weeks, and tell you when something isn't worth building.

PEC registered

Qualified engineers, not whoever was free this week.

Our software engineers are registered with the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), the statutory body that licenses professional engineers. Your system is designed by people with an accredited engineering degree and a professional code to answer to.

  • Accredited engineering degrees

    Registration needs a degree from a PEC-accredited engineering programme, not a weekend course.

  • A professional code of conduct

    Registered engineers are accountable to the council's code of ethics, on top of our contract with you.

  • Check it yourself

    Ask for the registration numbers of the engineers on your project. We're happy to share them.

Remote by default. On site when it matters.

Not every problem lives in the code. Sometimes it's the receipt printer, the network in the back office or a server under the stairs. We solve the physical side too, so you don't have to coordinate three different vendors.

  • Hardware setup. POS terminals, receipt printers, barcode scanners, kitchen screens, attendance devices.
  • Networks and servers. Wi-Fi and cabling, local servers, backups that actually run.
  • Go-live on the floor. An engineer with your staff on launch day, training in person.
  • Physical troubleshooting. When a remote fix isn't enough, we come and look.

On-site visits are planned per project. Tell us where you are and we'll tell you what's possible.

How we work with clients

Fixed quotes, in writing

You know the scope, timeline and price before work starts. If the scope changes, we agree the change in writing first.

You own what you pay for

Source code, data, accounts and domains are yours once the project is paid for. No lock-in.

Working software every two weeks

Not status reports. A link you can click, so you can steer early instead of being surprised at the end.

We adopt other people's code

Built by a freelancer or an agency that's gone quiet? We audit it, stabilise it and document it before we promise anything.

AI that's checked, not trusted blindly

Our agents work from your real data, follow written rules and hand off to a person when unsure. We test them on your actual customer questions.

We stay after launch

Every project includes a fix period after launch, and long-term maintenance contracts keep things running for years.

From first call to launch

  1. Week 0

    Discovery call

    A free 30 minute call about your business, the problem and what success looks like.

  2. Week 1

    Proposal and quote

    Scope, screens, timeline, milestones and a fixed price. Sometimes a short paid workshop first, for larger systems.

  3. Weeks 2+

    Design and build

    Two-week sprints. Each ends with a demo you can try and feedback we act on in the next sprint.

  4. Launch

    Go live

    Data migration, hardware setup if needed, staff training, written guides and close support during the first weeks.

  5. After

    Fix period and care

    Bugs found after launch are fixed free during the fix period. After that, a maintenance contract keeps things running.
