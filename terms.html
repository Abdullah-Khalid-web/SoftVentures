A critical issue means the system is down or a core function (such as taking orders or payments) is unusable for most users.

Included hours

Hours for changes are counted per calendar month and don't roll over unless your contract says so. Work beyond the included hours is billed at the rate in your contract, after your approval.

Not included

Large new features, redesigns, recovery from damage caused by others' changes, and third-party costs are not included unless stated in your plan.
