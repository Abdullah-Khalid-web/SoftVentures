What we fix

Websites, web apps, mobile apps, APIs and databases, whether we built them or not.

  • Bugs and errors. Crashes, wrong totals, features that stopped working after an update.
  • Slow performance. Pages that take seconds to load, reports that time out, apps that freeze.
  • Security problems. Hacked sites, exposed data, outdated libraries, weak logins.
  • Hosting and deployment. Failed deploys, expired certificates, servers that keep going down, moving hosts.
  • AI and automation issues. Chatbots giving wrong answers, agents stuck in loops, automations that fire twice or not at all. We fix the flow, not just the symptom.
  • Stalled projects. Code from a developer who disappeared. We review it, tell you what's usable and finish the job.

How a fix works

  1. 1

    You describe the problem

    What's happening, since when, and screenshots if you have them.

  2. 2

    We investigate

    With access to the code or system, we find the cause and how far it reaches.

  3. 3

    You approve the estimate

    A written explanation of the problem, the fix, the time and the cost. Nothing billable starts before you agree.

  4. 4

    We fix, test and report

    The fix is tested on a copy first, then released. You get a short report of what changed.
