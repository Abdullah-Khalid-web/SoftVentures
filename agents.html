What happens when a message arrives

  1. 1

    It reads the message

    Text, voice notes or a photo of a list, in the customer's own words and language.

  2. 2

    It checks your system

    Prices, stock, free slots and customer history come from your data, never from guesswork.

  3. 3

    It does the task

    Creates the order, books the slot or updates the record, then confirms with the customer.

  4. 4

    It knows when to stop

    Complaints, refunds and anything unclear go to a person on your team, with the chat attached.

Questions people ask before starting

How long does it take to launch an agent?

A focused agent, such as order taking for one restaurant, usually takes two to four weeks including testing with your staff. Agents that connect to several older systems take longer, and we'll tell you up front.

Do I need a special WhatsApp account?

Yes, agents run on the official WhatsApp Business Platform. We set it up with you on your own business account, usually keeping your existing number. Meta charges small per-conversation fees, which are billed to your account.

What if the agent gets something wrong?

Agents only quote prices, stock and slots from your system, and they confirm details before acting. Every conversation is logged so you can review it, and anything outside the agent's job is passed to a person.

Can it connect to the software I already use?

Usually, yes. If your software has an API we connect to it directly. If it doesn't, we can sync through its database or exports, or build you a management system that the agent works with.

What does it cost to run each month?

There are three parts: the platform fees from Meta, the AI usage for the messages, and hosting. We estimate all three in your proposal based on your message volume, and they can be included in a maintenance contract.
