The short version

  • This website doesn't use tracking cookies or advertising trackers.
  • When you contact us, we use your details only to reply and to work with you.
  • When we build or run systems and agents for a business, that business controls its customers' data. We process it only on their instructions.
  • We don't sell personal data.

This website

The website itself doesn't collect personal data through forms. The contact form opens your own email app or WhatsApp with your message filled in, so your message reaches us the same way as any other email or WhatsApp message.

We save one setting in your browser's local storage: whether you chose the light or dark theme. It never leaves your device. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. The site loads fonts and scripts from third-party networks (Google Fonts, the Tailwind CSS CDN and unpkg), which receive your IP address when they deliver those files.

Clients and enquiries

When you contact us or become a client, we collect what you give us: your name, email, phone number, business name, country, and the details of your project. We use this to reply, prepare proposals, deliver the work, send invoices and meet legal and accounting obligations. Our basis is your request, our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in running our business.

Data in systems we build or run

Systems and agents we build often hold personal data about our clients' customers, patients, students or staff. For that data, our client is the controller and decides how it's used. We act as a processor: we access it only to build, host, support or fix the system, follow our client's instructions, keep it confidential and help them respond to requests from the people concerned.

If you're a customer of one of our clients and have a question about your data, please contact that business directly. We'll help them answer.

Where an agent uses an AI model provider, messages are sent to that provider only to generate a reply. We choose providers and settings that don't use your data to train their models, where such options exist.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers we need to do our work, such as hosting and cloud providers, email and messaging platforms, AI model providers, and payment and accounting tools. They may only use it to provide their service to us. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

International transfers

We're based in Pakistan and work with clients worldwide, and our providers may store data in other countries. When we transfer personal data across borders, we use providers with appropriate safeguards and, where required (for example under the UK or EU GDPR), standard contractual clauses or equivalent protections.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access control with individual accounts, two-factor authentication on our tools, regular backups and the principle of least access. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data, we'll inform you and, where required, the relevant authorities, without undue delay.

How long we keep data

Enquiries that don't lead to a project are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for the length of our relationship and then as long as tax and accounting law requires. Data in client systems is kept, returned or deleted as our client instructs when our contract ends.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us. We'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children through this website. School and university systems we build handle student data only on behalf of the institution, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will be communicated to clients directly.
