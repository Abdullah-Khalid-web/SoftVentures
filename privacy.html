The short version

Here are the main things you should know about how SoftVentures handles personal information:

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We use information you provide to respond to enquiries, manage projects and deliver services.
  • Our website uses local browser storage for your theme preference.
  • We may receive basic technical information through our hosting and website service providers.
  • When we build or maintain systems for businesses, those businesses generally decide how their customers' information is used.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect the information entrusted to us.

1. This website

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be processed by the hosting provider and services used to deliver website content.

Depending on the hosting configuration, this information may include your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and access times. Such information may be used for website operation, troubleshooting and security.

Contact methods

When you contact us through email or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and message. We use it to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you.

Cookies and local storage

Our website may use browser local storage to remember your selected light or dark theme. This preference is stored in your browser and is not intended to identify you.

We do not intentionally use advertising tracking cookies on this website. However, third-party services used to deliver fonts, scripts or other website resources may process technical information, including your IP address.

2. Clients and enquiries

When you contact us, request a quotation or become a client, we may collect information you provide, including:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your business or organization name.
  • Your country or general business location.
  • Project requirements and technical information.
  • Communication, correspondence and support requests.
  • Billing and transaction information necessary to manage payments.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, provide services, manage projects, issue invoices, provide support and meet applicable legal obligations.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, our legal basis for processing may include taking steps before entering a contract, performing a contract, complying with legal obligations or pursuing legitimate business interests.

3. Personal data in systems we build or maintain

Software systems we develop may be used by businesses, educational institutions and other organizations to manage information about their customers, employees, students, patients or other individuals.

In these situations, the organization using the system generally determines why and how personal information is processed. That organization is typically the data controller, while SoftVentures may act as a data processor or service provider, depending on the arrangement and applicable law.

Where we act as a processor, we handle personal data according to the agreed service arrangement and the client's documented instructions.

This may involve accessing information to develop, configure, host, maintain, troubleshoot or support the system.

If you are a customer, student, employee or other individual whose information is held in a system operated by one of our clients, please contact that organization directly regarding your personal data.

AI-powered features

Some systems may use artificial intelligence services to process information and generate responses, extract document data or automate specific tasks.

Where an external AI provider is used, relevant information may be transmitted to that provider to perform the requested operation. The applicable provider, processing settings and data handling arrangements depend on the project and service configuration.

We aim to use appropriate provider settings and contractual safeguards where available. Clients should review the AI provider's terms and data processing arrangements applicable to their system.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our business or deliver our services.

These providers may include:

  • Website hosting and cloud infrastructure providers.
  • Email and communication service providers.
  • Messaging platforms and API providers.
  • AI and document-processing service providers.
  • Payment processors and financial service providers.
  • Technical support and security service providers.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal requests or to protect our legal rights and the security of our services.

5. International data transfers

SoftVentures operates from Pakistan and may work with clients and service providers in different countries.

As a result, personal information may be processed or stored outside the country where it was originally collected.

Where applicable, we aim to use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, which may include contractual protections or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms required by relevant data protection laws.

6. How we protect information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Depending on the service and project, these measures may include:

  • Encrypted connections where supported.
  • Access controls and role-based permissions.
  • Individual user accounts and authentication controls.
  • Restricted access to project data and infrastructure.
  • Backups and recovery procedures where configured.
  • Monitoring and maintenance of supported systems.

No internet-based service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps to investigate, contain and respond to it, including notifications where required by law or contract.

7. How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Enquiries may be retained for as long as needed to respond and manage follow-up communication.
  • Client and project records may be retained during the business relationship and for applicable legal, tax and accounting requirements.
  • Support and technical records may be retained as needed to maintain and troubleshoot services.
  • Information held in client-operated systems is managed according to the applicable client agreement and instructions.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to legal, contractual and technical limitations.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting access to your personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of information where applicable.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing activities.
  • Requesting a copy of information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

If we process your information on behalf of one of our clients, we may refer your request to that organization.

9. Children's privacy

Our website and general business services are primarily intended for businesses and organizations.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website.

Some software systems we develop may be used by schools, universities or other institutions that handle student information. In such cases, the relevant institution is responsible for determining the lawful basis and appropriate handling of that information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, business practices or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date at the top.

Where appropriate, significant changes may also be communicated directly to affected clients.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, how we handle personal information or wish to submit a privacy request, contact us.
